## English

I'm sharing the flashing tools I use myself. I put them together with help from AI, based on my own experience with Android flashing.

Flashing always comes with some risk. Choosing the wrong image, slot, or partition can erase your data or even leave your phone unable to boot. Check everything carefully before you start, back up important data, and have a recovery plan ready. I can't guarantee these tools will work on your device. If something goes wrong, you're responsible for the outcome.

A brief introduction to each tool:
- [Fastbootd Payload tool](fastbootd/readme.md)
- [Recovery Payload tool](recovery/readme.md)
